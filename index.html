About me

I’m Yuyue Liu, but you can call me Marie. I’m an interaction designer working across digital products, physical prototypes and interactive storytelling. My process is research-led: I start from how people actually feel and behave, then turn those insights into experiences that are clear, useful and emotionally resonant.

I’m currently pursuing an MFA in Interaction Design at the School of Visual Arts in New York.

Focus · Interaction design, UX research, prototyping, 3D and real-time media
Tools · Figma, Blender, Unreal Engine, Arduino / ESP32, Python
Languages · English, Mandarin Chinese
